Decision Receipt

Built on a zero-dependency Monte Carlo engine that carries the loan in its own currency, charges family capital against the degree, and compares every path against not going at all. Levers are evaluated with common random numbers, so the deltas are the lever and not simulation noise. Known limitations — no green-card backlog, no post-hire unemployment, no non-completion — all push the same way: the true left tail is fatter than what you see here.
